Privacy Policy

Product Information Clipper

This Privacy Policy explains how Product Information Clipper collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the browser extension.

Information We Collect

The extension may access or store the following information:

  • Product information visible on the web page you choose to process, such as title, image, URL, price, description, attributes, and warranty details.
  • Authentication data, such as an API token, after you sign in through the extension.
  • Temporary locally stored field selections you make while using the manual picker.
  • Data you choose to export as a JSON file or submit to your connected backend service.

How We Use Information

We use information only to provide the extension’s core functionality, including:

  • Analyzing the active page to extract product information after you open the extension.
  • Allowing you to review, edit, and manually select product fields from the current page.
  • Saving your login session locally so you do not need to sign in every time.
  • Exporting product data as a JSON file when you choose Export.
  • Sending product data to your connected backend service when you choose Save.

When Information Is Collected

The extension is intended to work when you actively open it on a product page. It does not require you to provide information unless you choose to use the extension’s features, such as logging in, extracting data, manually picking fields, exporting JSON, or submitting data to your backend service.

Local Storage

The extension stores some information locally in your browser, including:

  • Your API token after login.
  • Temporary manual picker values while you are working with extracted fields.

This local storage is used only to support the extension’s functionality and is not sold or shared for advertising purposes.

Data Sharing

The extension may send product data and authentication information to the backend service configured for the extension when you explicitly choose to sign in or save a product. The extension does not sell your personal information.

Third-Party Services

If the extension connects to a backend API or related services operated by your organization or service provider, those services may process the information you submit through the extension according to their own privacy practices.

Data Retention

Locally stored data remains in your browser until it is removed, overwritten, or cleared by you or by the extension during normal operation. Data submitted to your backend service is retained according to that service’s policies.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the extension. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

  • You can stop using the extension at any time.
  • You can remove the extension from your browser.
  • You can log out to remove the stored session token used by the extension.
  • You can choose whether to export data locally or submit data to your backend service.

Children’s Privacy

This extension is not intended for children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will post the updated version at the same URL and revise the effective date below.
